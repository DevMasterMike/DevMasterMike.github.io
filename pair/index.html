Sign In Your Apple TV

This code came from Modern IPTV on an Apple TV. Open it in Modern IPTV on this iPhone, and the TV signs in a few seconds later.

Open in Modern IPTV

Don’t have Modern IPTV on this iPhone?

You don’t need it. On the Apple TV, choose Sign In on This Apple TV and enter the login your TV service gave you. Your iPhone offers its keyboard when you pick a box, so there’s no typing with the remote.

Already a member?

An Apple TV on the same Apple Account as your iPhone signs in and picks up your membership by itself. On any other Apple TV, choose Subscribe or Restore Purchases there.

Nothing on this page is sent anywhere. The code only means something to the Apple TV that showed it, and only for ten minutes.
